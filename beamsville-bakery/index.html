Since
1958

One bakery, three generations

The doors opened in Beamsville's farming community in 1958. The Salverda family took over in 1974 and never changed what mattered.

1958

The doors open

A bakery starts serving the small farming community of Beamsville, Ontario — traditional European baking, made fresh daily.

1974

The Salverdas take over

Klaas Salverda buys the bakery and brings Dutch baking traditions with him — suikerbrood, gevulde koeken, gebakjes — recipes that still run the ovens today.

Today

Benjamin & Bauke carry it on

The third generation keeps the same counter, the same recipes, and the same early mornings. Regulars drive in from Toronto for the donuts.

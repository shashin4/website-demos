For more than 25 years, Black Forest Pastry Shop has been part of Oakville’s Lakeshore community. The family-run bakery brings European baking traditions together with the cakes, cookies, tarts and savoury favourites customers come in for.

Stop by the shop to see the day’s selection, pick up something for the table, or call ahead with a question.

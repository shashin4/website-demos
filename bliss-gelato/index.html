What rolls in with the cart

One booking, everything handled. You pick the flavours and the date — we handle the rest, from setup to the last scoop.

  • ✓Up to 6 flavours served from the cart, chosen by you
  • ✓Cups, cones, spoons and napkins included
  • ✓Uniformed scooper for the full service
  • ✓Delivery, setup and teardown anywhere in the Hamilton area
  • ✓Cart signage and flavour board dressed to your event
  • ✓Vegan and dairy-free sorbetti so no guest is left out

Built for the big days

Three kinds of events make up most of our calendar. Each one gets a cart experience shaped around it — not a generic dessert table.

Weddings

The dessert your photographer keeps shooting

The cart becomes part of the décor — dressed in your colours, serving flavours your guests talk about on the drive home. A late-night gelato break beats a stale dessert table every time.

Couples can name a flavour for the day — a signature scoop on the board with your names on it.
Corporate

Client events and team days that feel different

Product launches, staff appreciation, holiday parties — a gelato cart pulls a crowd and keeps it. We serve fast, look sharp, and work around your run-of-show.

Branded cups and a custom flavour menu with your company name are available for corporate bookings.
Parties & festivals

Birthdays, graduations, community days

Backyard milestone or a main-street festival — the cart draws a line and the line draws a crowd. Dairy-free and vegan sorbetti mean every guest gets a scoop.

Smaller gatherings can book a gelato bar setup instead of the full cart — ask when you call.

Rick & Michelle

The Bluebird Café and Grill belongs to Rick and Michelle Arsenault, and you will usually find them on the floor, greeting guests the way they have for years. Their rule has never changed: every dish is prepared in-house and fresh to order, from a kitchen that treats scratch cooking as the only cooking.

The room sits in the heart of downtown Orangeville, the kind of place where the owners know the regulars and the regulars bring their friends. Wood-fired flavour, generous pours, and the steady hum of a dining room that has been doing this since 1982. Come hungry, leave happy.

Rick & Michelle Arsenault, owners

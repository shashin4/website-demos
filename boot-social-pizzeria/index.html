Why "The Boot"?

A local Italian restaurant and patio lounge on Kerr Street, serving true authentic Neapolitan-style thin crust pizzas, pastas and tapas — where the integrity of the ingredients is all that matters.

A taste of true heritage from old-world Italy. Or, as the rest of the world knows us… The Boot.

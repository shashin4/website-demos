Our story

One family. One specialty. Fifty years.

Since 1974, the Bourque family has done one thing and done it well: transmissions. We're York Region's driveline specialists — our experienced technicians rebuild and repair transmissions on both domestics and imports, from everyday automatics to 4×4s.

Transmission work is complicated and expensive, so we keep it simple: computer diagnosis to find the real problem, straight answers, and work done right. That's why so much of our business is return customers and their referrals.

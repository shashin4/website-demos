Why Brian's

We know you've got choices.
Here's why drivers stay.

There are plenty of auto repair shops in the Halton Hills area, and we're grateful every time a driver gives us a look. So here's our deal: we fix only what needs fixing, we tell you the truth even when it's a smaller job, and we back our work with the kind of pride that comes from 39 years on the same corner.

  • Accurate, honest assessments before any work starts
  • Latest diagnostic tools with old-school mechanical know-how
  • Competitive prices — quality work at a reasonable price
  • Tires, alignments, brakes, safety inspections and more, all in-house

A family shop, kept in the family

Brian and Aileen Bell opened these bays beside the Sunoco station back in December 1987 and ran them for nearly thirty-one years.

Yaser Bitar apprenticed under Brian, earned his Red Seal trade licence in 2010 — and when the Bells were ready to travel, Yaser bought the business in 2019, keeping the name Milton already knew.

— Same corner. Same standards.

Milton history

Our story

Good fish. Proper chips. A familiar welcome.

Bronte Fish & Chips has been family-run since 1968 and welcomes Oakville diners at 2313 Lakeshore Road West.

The menu keeps the focus where it belongs: British fish-and-chip favourites, seafood dinners and a classic dessert to finish.

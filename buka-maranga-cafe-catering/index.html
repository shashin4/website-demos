Our story

An independent café with a cult following.

Tucked into Millcreek Drive, Buka Maranga is the kind of neighbourhood spot people drive across Mississauga for — a café, bakery and caterer all under one roof.

The signatures say it all: the Buka Bagel Sammie, the show-stopping Lavender Blue Matcha Latte, hearty lentil soup and power bowls built for real appetites. Everything is made fresh, in-house, by people who genuinely love feeding people.

Indian-fusion cafe on Broadway, Orangeville

Where coffee meets community

Cafetown is the cafe Orangeville treats like its living room. Brewed coffee, fresh pastries, and a warm room where regulars are known by name. The menu celebrates Indo-Canadian tradition and invention in the same bite.

Founded by Sahil and Gurleen, who set out to build a cafe where two food cultures meet and everybody belongs. It worked: 935 reviewers rate it 4.90, making it one of the top rated cafes in town.

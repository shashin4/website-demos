Why the fritter?

Because a fritter should be an event, not a pastry-case afterthought. Ours are mixed, fried and glazed to order — crisp outside, soft apple inside, gone in about four bites.

It's the thing we're known for across Waterdown, and honestly, it's the reason half our regulars walked in the first time.

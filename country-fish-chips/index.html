Our Story

Port Credit’s beloved little chippy

Tucked into 293 Lakeshore Rd E, right where the Credit River meets Lake Ontario, Country Fish & Chips has become a fixture of the Port Credit strip — the kind of place neighbours recommend to neighbours.

Done the old-fashioned way

Fish battered to order, fries cut fresh, and the classics that make a chip shop a chip shop — mushy peas, fried mushrooms, and a proper seaside spread. No shortcuts, no fuss.

Loved by the locals

With a 4.45-out-of-5 rating from around 844 reviews, this is the spot Port Credit trusts when the craving for golden, crispy fish & chips hits.

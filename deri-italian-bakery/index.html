Our Story

Three generations of Italian baking on Bloor Street.

Since 1976, Deri has been a family-run bakery, deli and caterer serving Mississauga — baking the old way, with flour, time and patience.

Our ovens turn out fresh focaccia and breads every morning. Our kitchen rolls out lasagna, penne alla vodka and cannelloni the way Nonna made them. And our deli counter and cake studio have been at the centre of birthdays, weddings and Sunday dinners for nearly five decades.

We're not a chain — we're your neighbours. When you call, you talk to family.

— The Deri Family

Two rooms, one visit

Browse, then stay for lunch

i. The shelves

A proper little bookshop — new and secondhand finds, staff picks, and the kind of browsing where you came in for one book and leave with four.

"Inexpensive" is the word regulars use — and they mean it as a compliment.

Ask Darren or Jeanette what's good this week. They'll have an opinion, and it'll be right.

Your hosts

Darren & Jeanette

"This is the kind of place where the owners remember your order and your reading list."

Darren and Jeanette run the Dog-Eared Café the way a neighbourhood bookshop-café should be run: personally. They host, they recommend, they're behind the counter — and regulars come back as much for them as for the coffee.

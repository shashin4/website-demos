What we do

Full-service repair, German-car fluent

From routine maintenance to deep engine work — on German makes first, and every other make and model too. We only do the work your car actually needs.

01

Engine diagnostics

Factory-level diagnostic software built for the electronics in your vehicle — we find the real fault instead of guessing at parts.

02

Lube, oil & filters

Proper-spec oil and filters for European engines, on a schedule matched to how you actually drive.

03

Brakes

Pads, rotors and full brake system work — if you hear grinding, don’t wait. Worn pads chew through rotors fast.

04

Tires & wheels

Seasonal changeovers, balancing and wheel service to keep the car planted through a Waterloo Region winter.

05

Air conditioning

Diagnosis and repair for weak or warm A/C — compressors, condensers and refrigerant, done right.

06

Belts & hoses

Timing belts, drive belts and hoses replaced on interval — the cheap insurance that prevents the expensive failure.

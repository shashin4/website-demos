Our Story

One goal since 1994: a genuinely enjoyable night out.

We started in 1994 with a single idea — that dinner should feel like an occasion, not an errand.

Three decades and four locations later, that idea still runs the kitchen. Our pizzas come out of a real stone oven, our ingredients are fresh, and we cook for every kind of appetite — the classics people grew up on and the dishes they come back for.

Thanks to loyal customers across Hamilton and Burlington, we've grown from one dining room to four. But every location still cooks like the first one did.

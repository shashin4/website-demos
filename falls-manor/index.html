The house legend

Niagara's original home of broasted chicken.

Broasting combines pressure cooking with deep frying: the chicken is marinated and breaded, then cooked under pressure so it comes out crunchy outside and juicy inside, sealed in its own natural juices.

Guests call it the best broasted chicken around, hands down, and pair it with broasted potatoes, pierogi with onions and bacon, or a stack of golden dipped pancakes. Walk-ins are welcome, and takeout is available if you would rather eat at home.

What "broasted" means

Pressure cooking meets deep frying.

  1. Marinated and breadedChicken seasoned and breaded the Falls Manor way.
  2. Pressure cookedThe pressure seals in natural juices and nutrition while keeping oil out.
  3. Served goldenCrunchy on the outside, juicy all the way through.

Large groups

Bring the whole bus.

Walk-ins are welcome. Reservations are for large groups of 20 or more only. To book the private room for your group, call (905) 358-3211. The private room is not available Saturday or Sunday.

Special rates and reservations are offered for church groups, senior citizen groups, and international visitor groups. Call ahead with your group size and dates, and the team will arrange the details with you.

A 50% deposit reserves your group booking, with the balance paid on check-in. Cancel 48 hours before your date for a full refund. Groups need two weeks notice to cancel.

There is plenty of motor coach parking on site. A rooming list is required two weeks before arrival. There is no charge for baggage handling.

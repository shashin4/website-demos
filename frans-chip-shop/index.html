Kingston’s chippy for 30+ years.

Fran’s opened in the early nineties and became a west-end regular favourite — the kind of place people drive across town for. In 2014, long-time employee Maggie Bender took the business into her own hands and kept the regulars coming back.

The dining room’s been renovated since, but the formula hasn’t changed: battered-to-order fish, a real fryer, and food wrapped the old way.

The family table

How a Great Wall dinner works.

Chinese dinner is a team sport. Here's the play:

  1. 1

    Order for the middle

    One dish per person, plus rice — but nothing belongs to anyone. It all lands in the middle.

  2. 2

    Mix Cantonese with Szechuan

    Silky BBQ pork next to something with heat. The contrast is the point.

  3. 3

    Pass it around

    Everyone tastes everything. The best bite at a Chinese table is always someone else's dish.

Feeding a crowd? Call ahead and we'll have it hot and ready when you arrive — dine in or takeout.

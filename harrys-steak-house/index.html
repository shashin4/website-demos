Signature cuts & classics

Dry-aged in house, cut thick, and finished over real fire. These are the plates the regulars order on repeat.

  • Bone-In Dry-Aged Rib SteakHouse signature Aged for depth, cut on the bone, seared hard and rested properly.
  • Filet MignonPlain or bacon-wrapped The tenderest cut on the menu — take it classic, or wrapped in bacon.
  • Tomahawk Pork Chop For the non-beef loyalists: a bone-in chop with a serious sear.
  • Bone Marrow Roasted and spreadable — order it once and you'll order it always.
  • Steak Tartare Hand-cut, seasoned to order, served the traditional way.
  • Shrimp Cocktail Chilled jumbo shrimp, sharp cocktail sauce. The old-school opener.
  • French Onion Soup Deep beef broth, a raft of gruyère, broiled until it bubbles.
  • Loaded Wedge Salad Iceberg wedge, bacon, blue cheese — the steakhouse salad, done right.
  • Baked AlaskaFor the table Torched meringue, ice cream, sponge — the showstopper finale.

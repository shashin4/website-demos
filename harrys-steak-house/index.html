The room

Dinner the way it used to be.

Art-deco dining rooms, dark wood, soft light, and white tablecloths — Harry's is an Old Hollywood room in the middle of Etobicoke, and it shows.

Service that remembers your name, steaks dry-aged in house, and a dining room with no chains and no shortcuts.

  • Dry-aged in house — steaks aged for flavour, seared over real fire.
  • Independent — one Etobicoke dining room, run by people who work here.

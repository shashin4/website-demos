Since 1955

Seventy years on the same street.

Henry’s opened its doors in 1955 and never really left — the oldest traditional barbershop in the city of Peterborough. The poles, the leather chairs, the hot lather: the ritual hasn’t changed because it never needed to.

What did change is the room around it. The shop was rebuilt from two chairs to six, so more of downtown can get in the chair — without losing the unhurried, talk-it-over way a proper barbershop works.

  • 1955

    Henry’s opens on Hunter Street West — a traditional German-rooted barbershop.

  • 2010s

    Master barber Jay Dymond and partner Mike Macdonald take the legacy on and refurbish the shop.

  • Today

    Six chairs, walk-ins welcome, and the same straight-razor standards as day one.

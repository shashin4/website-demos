THE CORNER OF KENNEDY & ELLESMERE

Scarborough’s kind of Chinese restaurant.

Hakka cooking grew out of Chinese kitchens in India — high-heat wok work folded together with Indian spice. It’s garlicky, chili-forward, deeply savory, and built for the table.

Kim Kim Hakka serves that tradition on Kennedy Road: noodles and fried rice off a screaming-hot wok, chili-tossed appetizers, and plates meant to be passed around.

No fuss, no pretense — a neighborhood spot where the phone is the front door and dinner comes out of the wok minutes after you order. If you’re new to Hakka, start with the noodles. If you’re not new, you already know what to order.

HOUSE NOTE

The takeout box is half the menu.

That’s the honest truth at every great neighborhood Hakka spot: most of Scarborough’s Hakka orders leave in a paper bag. Call ahead and yours will be coming off the wok when you walk through the door.

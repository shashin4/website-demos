In the kitchen

Cooked by hand, by Chef Joanna.

Nothing at La Contessa comes off an assembly line. Every entree is individually prepared by Chef Joanna, using only the freshest ingredients, and cooked the way good Italian food demands: with time.

Keep in mind ‘good food’ takes time, so relax, enjoy the ambiance and have one of our signature cocktails.

This is a family owned and operated restaurant, and it eats like one. Generous portions, a warm room, and staff who give you time to enjoy your evening without ever feeling rushed.

Signature cocktails and a full bar round out the evening. Call for tonight's specials.

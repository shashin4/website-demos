Private events

A banquet hall built for Belleville's biggest days

Up to 90 guests, a full licensed bar, and a team that has hosted hundreds of weddings, anniversaries, and corporate evenings. This is the room the Quinte books when it matters.

  • Seats up to 90 guests, with podium, dance floor, projector screen, and full linen settings
  • Full licensed bar and free Wi-Fi throughout the building, ideal for business meetings and conferences
  • Weddings, rehearsals, birthdays, anniversaries, team sporting dinners, and corporate and civic functions
  • Main dining room separately licensed for 116 guests
  • Flexible menu, happy to work around dietary restrictions
  • Steps from the Country Inn & Suites, handy for out-of-town guests
Hall rental is complimentary with a group of 30 or more enjoying a meal. Dates for wedding season go first, so call early.

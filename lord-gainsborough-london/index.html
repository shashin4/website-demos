About the house

Lord Gainsborough is a family-style restaurant in London's Hyde Park, cooking across three traditions: Greek, pizza, and Canadian diner classics.

It's the kind of place with something for every table, from a morning breakfast to a Friday-night pizza run, with pickup and delivery for when you'd rather eat at home.

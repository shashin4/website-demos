Your home away from home

Walk into Louie's and you're greeted by name — that's just how Dina and Louie run things. This is a family diner in the truest sense: the food is home-cooked, the servings are generous, and the room is full of neighbours who came in for breakfast years ago and never really left.

From a proper eggs benedict to start your morning, to burgers and sandwiches at lunch, everything comes out hot, fast, and made with care. Small room, big heart — that's Louie's.

The Louie's promise

Delicious and affordable all-day breakfast, lunch specials with real home cooking, and a kids' menu that makes the whole family happy.

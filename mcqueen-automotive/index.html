Word of mouth built this shop

Lennie Silvestri opened McQueen Automotive in 1996 and never advertised much — he never had to. Satisfied customers sent their neighbours, their coworkers, their families. Nearly 30 years on, word-of-mouth referrals are still his largest source of new business.

Hundreds of Brampton residents have come to rely on Lennie and his crew to keep their family vehicles in peak running condition — good, old-fashioned customer service, job after job.

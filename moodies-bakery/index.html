Our story

A downtown institution, one loaf at a time.

Moodie's has been bringing freshly baked goods to Niagara Falls for 90 years: breads, pastries, cookies, cakes, and the authentic Canadian butter tart, made with quality ingredients and a passion for great taste.

Out and about

Find us at the Saturday markets.

Alongside the Queen Street shop, Moodie's sets up at farmers' markets on Saturdays. Fresh bread, butter tarts, and pastries, straight from the oven to the stall.

  • Every
    Sat

    Niagara Falls Farmers' Market

    Saturdays, in the morning. Look for the Moodie's table for breads and butter tarts.

  • Every
    Sat

    Welland Farmers' Market

    Saturdays, in the morning. Breads, pastries, and the week's cake selection.

Market days can shift with the season. Call 905 358 8288 to confirm where to find us this Saturday.

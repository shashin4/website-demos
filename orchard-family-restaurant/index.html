Our Story

Sixty years. One family. Zero shortcuts.

Orchard Family Restaurant opened its doors in 1966 — and Mississauga has been coming back ever since. What started as a family dream is still family-run today by Tom Velianou, son and nephew of the founders, who keeps the same promise the first generation made: honest food, generous portions, fair prices.

This is the kind of place where breakfast is served all day, the coffee is always hot, and the pies and pastries come out of the oven every single morning. A true Mississauga institution — the family table the whole city shares.

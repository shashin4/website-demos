1981Family-owned since

Four decades on Front Street

Jerry Kostur opened the bakery in 1981, and the Kostur family, Lisa and their three sons, has run it ever since. What started as a neighbourhood bakery now smokes its own deli meats, bakes for local restaurants, and caters events across the Orillia area.

The recipe hasn't changed: bake it fresh, smoke it yourself, slice it to order.

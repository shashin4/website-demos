Vera's kitchen

Verica K. Gvozdenovic — Vera to her regulars — is the owner and restaurateur behind Queen Gypsy, and has been for over ten years. It is her cooking that made a little café-diner on Main Street South one of downtown Brampton's favourite tables.

The room is warm and eclectic, the portions generous, the service quick for a small crew that gets very busy some nights. On the plate: slow, hearty Eastern European cooking — gypsy stew, goulash, cabbage rolls, schnitzel — and from the bakery case, challah, golden turnovers, and the cinnamon buns people cross town for.

On Saturdays the café turns market. More on that below — it's worth planning around.

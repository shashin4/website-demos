Roasted for Red Brick, named for Guelph

Our beans are roasted by Planet Bean and served as certified organic and fair-trade coffee — including Swiss Water Process decaf. Each blend carries a name from our neighbourhood.

☕

Blue Dog

Medium roast

Our signature medium — smooth, balanced, and the backbone of our espresso drinks.

☕

Douglas St.

Dark roast

Named for our own street. Bold and deep for those who like their coffee with backbone.

☕

Speed River

Espresso

Our espresso blend — rich and lively, built for flat whites, lattes and cappuccinos.

Fair trade means the farmers are paid properly for their work. Organic means the coffee is grown without synthetic pesticides. We think you can taste the difference — and so can the planet.

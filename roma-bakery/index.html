BAKING EVER SINCE

A Stoney Creek original

Roma Bakery opened its doors in 1952 and never really closed them — three generations later, it’s still the same neighbourhood bakery on Barton Street.

Today it’s run by Philip Di Filippo and his son Anthony, carrying on the recipes and the routine: early mornings, full ovens, and regulars who’ve been coming in for decades.

That’s why the slab pizza is famous. Not a trend — just seventy-plus years of doing the same thing right.

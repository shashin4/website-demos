What to expect

Bringing your car in

Most people dread the repair shop because nobody tells them what happens next. Here’s the whole thing, in order.

Step one

You tell us what’s wrong

Call or drop by at 205 John St S. Describe the noise, the light on the dash, the leak — in your own words, no car talk required.

Step two

We look, then we call you

We inspect the car and ring you with what’s actually wrong, what it costs, and what can wait. Nothing starts without your say-so.

Step three

You approve, we fix

One price agreed up front. If we find something else mid-job, we stop and call before touching it.

Step four

You pick it up, we walk you through it

We explain what was done and why, in plain language. If anything feels off afterwards, bring it back and say so.

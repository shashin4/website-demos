Every pet, groomed right

Pick your pet to see what we do. Dogs get the full spa treatment, cats get a calm dog-aware approach, and pocket pets are very welcome here too.

Dog grooming & spa

All breeds, shapes and sizes, from bath-and-fluff to full custom cuts.

  • Full grooms · bath, fluff dry, cut and finish
  • Breed-specific & custom cuts · including mohawks and lion cuts
  • De-skunking · skunk odour treatment that actually works
  • Hotspot treatment · careful shave and cleaning of irritated areas
  • Ear cleaning & nail trims · part of every groom or on their own

A short, fun stay

Safety comes first here. Pets are never kept longer than needed, and we call you the moment the groom is finished so pickup is quick and stress-free.

Cat grooming

Patient, cat-savvy grooming for felines who deserve better than a stressful salon day.

  • Cat shaving & grooming · full coats and tidy-ups
  • Lion cuts · the classic summer favourite
  • Brush-outs · de-matting and coat care
  • Cat nail trims · quick and gentle
  • Sanitary trims · comfort and hygiene

Prefer to stay home?

Many cats do. Our in-home service brings nail trims, brush-outs and sanitary trims to your door, for cats, dogs, ferrets and guinea pigs. Call to inquire.

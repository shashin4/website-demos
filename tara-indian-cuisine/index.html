Our story

Barrie's finest, by word of mouth

When Yar and Nur Mohammad opened Tara Indian Cuisine in 1989, they cooked the way they'd always cooked — and Barrie noticed. Word of mouth carried their name well beyond Dunlop Street; over the years their following has stretched to customers visiting from as far as Europe.

The brothers are easy-going hosts who happily adjust a dish to suit your palate — milder, hotter, vegetarian, or something in between. That personal touch is why so many Barrie families have been coming back for decades.

Fully licensed, warmly lit, and unhurried: this is the room for anniversary dinners, family celebrations, and the Tuesday night you don't feel like cooking.

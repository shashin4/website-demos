1983

Four decades of
rolling pastry.

That Pie Place is a family-run Burlington bakery that has been making savoury pies since 1983. What started at the family counter grew into something bigger: since 1993 we've supplied our pies wholesale to local retailers across the area.

Through all of it, the method hasn't changed. Pastry rolled by hand. Fillings made from scratch. The kind of pie people drive across town for — and have, for forty years.

  • TodaySame hand-rolled pastry, same family standards

WHY THEY TASTE LIKE THAT

No shortcuts in the crust.

Tenderflake-lard pastry

Our crusts are hand-rolled with Tenderflake lard — the old-fashioned way. That's the flake you can't get from a factory shell.

Chicken in its own stock

Our chicken pies are made with chicken cooked down in its own stock. Rich, real gravy — never a packet.

Baked through, not rushed

Bottom crusts fully baked, fillings seasoned to taste, pies cooled properly. Forty years teaches you patience.

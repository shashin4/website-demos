Our story

A family counter for over three decades.

Tre Rose Bakery has been family-owned and run on Kipling Ave since 1989 — an Italian bakery, café and deli that has fed the neighbourhood for more than three decades.

Regulars come in for the morning bread and stay for the coffee. Walk in any day of the week — the ovens run every morning and the doors are open 10 AM–7 PM, seven days.

Family-owned An independent Etobicoke bakery — not a chain, not a franchise.

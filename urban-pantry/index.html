Hours

When we're here

Two seatings most days — a midday lunch and an evening dinner — plus a long Sunday brunch. Closed Monday and Tuesday, when the kitchen rests and the farms get a visit.

Farm to table

The season is the menu

We cook what's growing. That means the menu you read in October won't be the one you read in March — and that's the point.

As a FeastOn-certified kitchen, we source from Ontario farms first, and chef Claudio Briones builds the plate around what arrives that week. Owner Niki Pagidas runs the room the same way: personal, unhurried, and rooted in Uxbridge.

Because everything turns on the harvest, we don't publish a fixed menu online. Call (905) 852-5050 and we'll walk you through today's features — what's on the lunch board, what the kitchen is proudest of tonight, and what to save room for.

Planning around a visit? Lunch runs 11:30 AM–2:30 PM Wednesday through Saturday, dinner from 4:30 PM. Sunday is brunch from 9 AM, then dinner service from 4:30 PM.

Sundays

Brunch, the slow way

Sunday mornings at the Pantry run 9 AM to 2 PM — a proper brunch built from the same farm-first kitchen as dinner. Then the room resets and dinner service starts at 4:30 PM.

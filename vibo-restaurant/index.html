La storia

Fifty years at the same stove.

In 1972, our family opened its doors in the Kingsway as Villa Borghese. In 2012 we became ViBo — but the philosophy never changed.

Real Italian cooking, generous portions, and a room that feels like family. Pasta made in house, sauces that simmer all afternoon, and recipes carried across three generations without losing a thing.

Casual fine dining on Bloor West: the white-tablecloth care of a special occasion, the warmth of a neighbourhood spot where the staff knows your name.

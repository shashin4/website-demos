Dal 1972

Three generations, one kitchen.

We opened our doors as Villa Borghese in 1972 and became ViBo in 2012 — but the philosophy never changed: real Italian cooking, generous portions, and a room that feels like family.

  • 1972Villa Borghese opens its doors in the Kingsway.
  • 2012Reborn as ViBo — same family, same recipes, new name.
  • TodayHandmade pasta, veal chops, and branzino, seven days a week.

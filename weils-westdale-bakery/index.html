Heritage

A long established Hamiltonian tradition.

Weil’s of Westdale has been baking in Hamilton long enough to become part of the neighbourhood’s routine — the smell of the ovens on King Street West, the same counter, the same care.

Over the years we’ve tried a lot of recipes from all over the world. We kept only the ones that food lovers keep coming back for — that’s the whole philosophy, and it hasn’t changed.

“Only the recipes worth coming back for.”

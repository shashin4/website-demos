What we bake

Everything made by hand, every morning.

Whether you’re after an artisan loaf, something sweet with your coffee, or a cake for a birthday — you’ll find lots to tempt your tastebuds at Weil’s of Westdale.

  • Artisan breadsBaked daily Slow-fermented loaves with real crust — the kind of bread that makes a meal.
  • Melt-in-your-mouth rollsCustomer favourite Soft, buttery rolls. Buy a bag — they won’t survive the drive home.
  • Pastries & breakfast treatsMornings Flaky, golden, and best eaten warm. Come early for the best pick.
  • CookiesBy the dozen Old fashioned cookies, baked in big batches all week.
  • CupcakesAssorted Moist, generously frosted, and gone by Saturday most weeks.
  • Good old fashioned loavesEveryday Simple sandwich bread, done right — soft crumb, honest flavour.

Custom cakes

Birthday or specialty cakes, made to order.

Tell us the occasion, the servings, and the flavours you’re dreaming of — we’ll bake a moist, delicious cake and finish it by hand. Every custom cake starts with a conversation.

Pricing depends on size and design — call for a quote.

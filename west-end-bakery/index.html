Our story

Two generations, one oven.

West End Bakery has been family owned and operated for two generations in the heart of downtown Guelph — and we’ve kept our original old-fashioned philosophy of quality and service the whole way.

We bake everything from scratch, with all-natural ingredients — just as if you were making it yourself. No shortcuts, no store-bought mixes.

And we’re very reasonably priced. Good baking shouldn’t cost a fortune.

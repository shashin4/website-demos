Our story

Fifty-seven years, one address.

Westown Auto is a family-run, 3-bay auto service centre in West Hamilton, Ontario — founded in 1969 and known ever since for delivering exceptional service without the runaround.

We specialize in import vehicles — the shop where drivers who are tired of dealership prices come for straight answers and work done right the first time.
